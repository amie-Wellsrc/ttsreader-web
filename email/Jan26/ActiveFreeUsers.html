2025 highlights
  • Universally Free and Unlimited tier: We are committed to keeping TTSReader accessible to everyone, with a robust free tier.
  • Outstanding value for Premium accounts: We focus on delivering more features and quality for every dollar you spend.
  • AI voices: Hundreds of new voices across 80+ languages, with more natural intonation and context-aware emphasis.
  • MP3 export: Generate long-form MP3 audio quickly and efficiently—share via link or download.
  • Dialogue integration: Smoother multi-speaker editing with our in-text editing workflow.
  • Podcast channel: Turn text into a private podcast feed you can listen to in any podcast app.
  • Find & Replace + Pronunciation Dictionary: Faster editing and better control over how words are spoken.
  • WikiTTS: 100,000+ Wikipedia articles available as audio for free public listening.

How TTSReader is being used in the real world

Education

In collaboration with educators and academia, we’ve seen TTSReader support reading comprehension and increase engagement, especially for learners with reading challenges such as dyslexia.

We remain committed to a completely free and unlimited tier so people who need TTSReader most can access it.

Jordan Cotten tech coach

Authors & writers

Work with authors and creators has helped accelerate our feature set and performance. Many creators use TTSReader to turn drafts into audiobooks and audio files—and some publish via podcast feeds.

Video & audio teams

Marketing teams use TTSReader for voiceovers and audio content. Our exports are used across platforms like YouTube and Instagram.

2026 Roadmap
  • TTSReader Chrome extension: Hundreds of new voices across 80+ languages, with more natural intonation and context-aware emphasis.
  • HD voices: Generate long-form MP3 audio quickly and efficiently—share via link or download.
  • Industry Collaboration: Smoother multi-speaker editing with our in-text editing workflow.
  • Voice Cloning: Many of our users need custom voices for branding and accessibility. In the near future we will offer voice cloning services for businesses and creators.
  • Emotional modulation: In early 2026, we plan to introduce emotionally aware, context-driven narration for professional users and creators.
